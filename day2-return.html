Corridor A

US-50 · El Dorado & Apple Hill

South Lake Tahoe → Placerville → Camino → Sacramento → Bay Area. The default drive home and the best-stocked one — this is where most of your color will come from.

🍁Apple Hill, Camino — shot Nov 2024 & Nov 2025 · Philip Reedy, California Fall Color
Best bet

Apple Hill · Camino

Off US-50, El Dorado County · 3,133 ft
Oct 18Nov 27

The single best reason to take US-50. Orchards, farms and vineyards throw red, orange and gold; the growers call early-to-mid November the "best-kept secret." Larsen Road is the classic fall drive.

Peak dates & sources
  • Apple Hill Growers: fall colors are best "the first two weeks of November." Apple Hill.

  • El Dorado Wines: the Camino / Apple Hill area shines late October through November. El Dorado Wines.

🍁Sierra Vista Winery, Pleasant Valley, El Dorado County — shot Nov 20, 2022 · John Poimiroo, California Fall Color
Best bet

El Dorado wine country

Fair Play · Camino · Pleasant Valley · ~2,000–2,800 ft
Oct 18Nov 27

The vines are the late show. After harvest the leaves explode into crimson, rust and gold and hold into mid-November — the photo above was shot Nov 20. Pair a stop with a tasting and a picnic.

Peak dates & sources
  • Photo: Sierra Vista Winery, Pleasant Valley (El Dorado County), Nov 20, 2022 — an actual vineyard in the region, late in the season. California Fall Color.
  • El Dorado Wines: "the grapevines themselves are autumn's unsung heroes… the leaves explode into vibrant hues of crimson, rust, and gold" — the foothill AVA shines late October through November. El Dorado Wines.
  • How the Nov 5–12 window was inferred. MTJP has no vineyard layer, so this is built from elevation. The county figure (El Dorado, peak Oct 18–20) is a canopy-weighted median that runs high, and the AVA sits lower at ~2,000–2,800 ft. In that band MTJP's own El Dorado NF page puts peak at Nov 5 (its 2,000–4,000 ft band). Vines color after the native canopy — post-harvest — and are documented into mid/late November (the Nov 20, 2022 photo above; grower guidance "late October through November"). So the peak is placed just past the Nov 5 band, at Nov 5–12, and it's set early on purpose: the photo evidence says color persists later, so your Nov 14–15 dates are still in play rather than past peak.
