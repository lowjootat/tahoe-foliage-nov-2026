Mid-November is late — here's what's left

Color moves downhill at roughly a day per 100 m (330 ft). By Nov 14–15 the high country is bare and most foothill forest is past peak, but the lowest, latest ground is still turning. Reports from recent seasons tell the story.

Tahoe & the foothill forest — done

Lake Tahoe's aspens are bare by now (they peak mid-October). The native forest of the El Dorado and Tahoe national forests — the black oaks and maples along Hwy 50 — peaked Oct 18–Nov 5 and is largely stripped.

Still, the lake itself is the reason you're there. Treat Day 1 as scenery, not foliage.

Vineyards, Apple Hill & the low valleys — still going

The grapevines are the late show: after harvest they blaze crimson, rust and gold into mid-to-late November across the El Dorado and Amador foothills. Apple Hill orchards are documented at full peak as late as Nov 18, and its own growers call early-to-mid November the "best-kept secret."

Low and last: valley oak, cottonwood and sycamore along the American and Mokelumne rivers, and Filoli's ginkgos near the Bay.

Trip basics

A quick weekend, so keep it simple: one big stop per half-day, and let the drive itself be the show.

Timing

  • Day 1 (Sat Nov 14) — Lake Tahoe: Emerald Bay, the South Shore, a lakeside walk
  • Day 2 (Sun Nov 15) — leave Tahoe after breakfast; two or three stops on the way down
  • Short days: sunset ~4:50 pm — front-load the scenic stops

What's actually turning

  • Grapevines — crimson, rust, gold (the reliable late show)
  • Orchards — Apple Hill apples, persimmon, maple
  • Low river trees — valley oak, cottonwood, sycamore
  • Planted town trees — pistache, liquidambar, ginkgo

Before you leave

  • Check the Caltrans QuickMap for Hwy 50 / I-80 / CA-88 conditions
  • A storm can strip the remaining leaves in a day — check the latest report
  • Tahoe passes can see early snow; carry chains
  • Confirm tasting-room hours; many are weekends-only in November
Weather fallback: if a storm snows in the passes or strips the foothills, drop lower and later — the Sacramento Valley's valley oaks and cottonwoods, and Filoli near the Bay, hold into late November.
